# EzReader privacy policy

*Effective 15 November 2026*

EzReader is a PDF reader and editor that works entirely on your computer. This policy explains what that means for your data.

## In short

EzReader does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no telemetry, no ads and no AI features. Your documents never leave your computer unless you send them somewhere yourself.

## Your documents

EzReader opens, shows and saves the files you choose, on your computer. It does not upload them, scan them for any other purpose, or keep copies elsewhere.

While you work, EzReader may make temporary working copies of a document in Windows' temporary folder (for example to show annotations, or to open a password-protected file). These copies are deleted when you close the app, or the next time it starts if it was closed unexpectedly.

## What EzReader stores on your computer

To remember your preferences between sessions, EzReader saves a few settings on your computer only: language, theme, default zoom, page brightness, screen calibration, whether the page panel is shown, the text-recognition language, and up to three signatures you choose to save. You can delete them by removing the app, or delete a saved signature from inside the app.

## Network access

EzReader does not connect to the internet on its own. The PDF viewer is locked down: documents cannot run scripts or reach the network. A web link opens in your browser only when you click it.

## Windows features

Text recognition (OCR) uses the engine built into Windows and runs on your computer. EzReader displays pages with Microsoft Edge WebView2, a Windows component. These Windows features are covered by Microsoft's own privacy statement, and EzReader sends them no data of its own.

If you install EzReader from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download and installation under its own privacy statement. EzReader does not receive any information about you from the Store.

## Children

EzReader collects no data from anyone, including children.
