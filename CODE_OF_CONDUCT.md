# Code of conduct

EzReader follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This page sums it up; the full text is the reference.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in EzReader to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect other opinions, points of view and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback, and own your mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use EzReader.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address, without their permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md), through a [private report on GitHub](https://github.com/alxbdn/EzReader/security/advisories/new) (mark it as a conduct issue) or by the contact details on their GitHub profiles. Every report is reviewed promptly and kept confidential. If a report is about a maintainer, the other maintainer handles it.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide on the response, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a private warning, a public warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending on how serious and how repeated the behavior is.
