Service Agreement

Reference SA-2026-0412 · 14 November 2026

1. Parties

This agreement is made between Atelier Nord, a design studio, and the Client named below. Together they agree on the scope, schedule and fees for the work described in section 2.

2. Scope of work

Atelier Nord will design a new visual identity for the Client, including a logo, a colour palette, typography guidelines and templates for print and screen. Two rounds of revisions are included.

3. Schedule

Work starts on 1 December 2026 and is delivered no later than 31 January 2027. Each milestone is reviewed together within five working days.

4. Fees and payment

The total fee is 4 800 EUR, paid in two instalments: 40 % on signature and 60 % on delivery. Invoices are payable within thirty days.

5. Ownership

On full payment, all rights to the final deliverables pass to the Client. Atelier Nord may show the work in its portfolio unless the Client objects in writing.
